Elevor360D Marketplace Pro V25.5 — automatic online photo optimisation

Select a JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF photo up to 25 MB. The app resizes it to a maximum of 1600 pixels along its longest edge, converts it to JPEG and compresses it below the 5 MB Supabase upload limit. Animated GIFs become still images; transparent areas are white. Original files remain unchanged on your device.
