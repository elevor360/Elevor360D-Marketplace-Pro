# Customer guide — Marketplace Pro V25

**Browse items:** Open **Menu → Browse** for device listings or **Cloud Listings** for online published listings. Search and filter as needed.

**Account:** Use **My Account** to sign up or sign in. Confirm your email if prompted. Online features require a connection.

**Selling:** Open **Sell an Item** to prepare a listing; use **My Listings** to manage local items and the online publishing controls to publish through Supabase. Only published online listings can be seen by other customers.

**Contacting sellers:** Open a listing's details and use the available contact or message controls. Check **Messages** and **Notifications** while the app is open. Notifications are not background push messages.

**Payment:** Buyers and sellers agree independently how to pay and exchange goods. The marketplace does not take payment, verify transfers or provide escrow. Never share passwords or one-time codes. Inspect goods and use a safe meeting place when appropriate.

**Saved records:** Favourites, local offers, payment notes and transaction records may be saved only on the current device. Export your listing backup and any separate CSV files before changing devices, uninstalling or clearing browser data.

**Privacy:** **Privacy & Security** can clear certain local records. It does not delete your Supabase account, cloud listings or messages. Contact the marketplace operator for online data requests.

**Updates:** Open **App Health & Updates** to check status. After a new release, refresh the website and reopen the installed app.
